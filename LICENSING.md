# Licensing ThetaScan

This page is a practical guide to the repository's license boundaries. It does
not replace the license texts and is not legal advice.

## Software

Unless a file says otherwise, ThetaScan software and its accompanying software
documentation are source-available under the
[PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](LICENSE), SPDX identifier
`PolyForm-Small-Business-1.0.0`.

The copyright holder and licensor of the original ThetaScan software covered by
this repository is **Ultimamind SRL, Belgium**. The public licensing contact is
[hi@aim.do](mailto:hi@aim.do).

For use of the software for the benefit of a company, the license's small
business permission applies only when **both** conditions are satisfied:

1. the company has fewer than 100 total employees and independent contractors;
   and
2. the company had less than USD 1,000,000 (2019) total revenue in its prior tax
   year, adjusted for inflation using the CPI measure specified in the license.

The definition of "your company" in [LICENSE](LICENSE) also covers organizations
under common control. Read the exact terms before relying on this summary. The
license does not state a blanket research, evaluation, educational, or nonprofit
exception from these conditions. Statutory rights such as fair use remain as
stated in the license.

PolyForm Small Business is a source-available license, not an OSI-approved Open
Source license. It has no future date on which ThetaScan automatically converts
to another license.

## Uses outside the standard license

If your use is not permitted by [LICENSE](LICENSE), you need a separate written
license before that use. This includes use for the benefit of a company that
does not satisfy both small-business conditions above.

Commercial and other alternative licenses can be discussed at
[hi@aim.do](mailto:hi@aim.do). No alternative license is granted until both
sides enter into a separate written agreement.

## Patents

The standard software license includes the patent grant and patent-defense
condition written in [LICENSE](LICENSE). [PATENTS.md](PATENTS.md) supplies the
repository's patent notice; it does not add to the license grant.

## Paper materials

The Public Preview v0.1 technical report is included under `paper/` for public reading and
citation under the collective byline **The ThetaScan Project**. It is not part
of the software or accompanying software documentation licensed under the
PolyForm Small Business terms. No separate paper-reuse license is currently
granted merely by its inclusion in this repository, and access to the paper
does not add patent rights. A separate copyright license can be stated in a
future paper version. The paper copyright and citation notice is summarized in
[`paper/README.md`](paper/README.md). Paper files are intentionally excluded
from the installable Python wheel. They are included, without any change to
their copyright status, in the official source distribution so that its
research claims and evidence links remain self-contained.

## Third-party material

Third-party components remain under their own terms. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). A third-party license does not
change the license for original ThetaScan code, and the ThetaScan license does
not replace a third party's terms.

## Required notice

Redistributions must preserve the official license terms or their URL and the
plain-text `Required Notice:` line supplied in [NOTICE](NOTICE), as required by
the Notices section of [LICENSE](LICENSE).
